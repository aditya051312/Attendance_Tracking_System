The tiger is a large and powerful animal. It is one of the most beautiful animals on the planet. Its coat is orange with black stripes that make it unique. Tigers are known for their strength and agility. They are apex predators and primarily eat meat. They are solitary animals and mark their territories. Tigers are found in various parts of Asia. Unfortunately, due to deforestation and poaching, the tiger population is decreasing. It is our responsibility to protect this majestic creature and its habitat. Let's pledge to save the tiger and respect nature.
