Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that aims to create intelligent machines. It has become an essential part of the technology industry.

Research associated with artificial intelligence is highly technical and specialized. The core problems of artificial intelligence include programming computers for certain traits such as:

- Knowledge
- Reasoning
- Problem-solving
- Perception
- Learning
- Planning
- Ability to manipulate and move objects

Artificial Intelligence is not just a part of our everyday life but is also a crucial player in the technology field. With its wide application and future possibilities, AI is a fascinating field.
