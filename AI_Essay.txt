Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that aims to create systems that can perform tasks that would normally require human intelligence. This includes tasks such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and language understanding. AI can be categorized into two types, Narrow AI which is designed to perform a narrow task and General AI which is an AI system with generalized human cognitive abilities. With advancements in machine learning and cognitive computing, the capabilities of AI are growing at an unprecedented rate. AI has a wide range of applications including in fields like healthcare, education, finance, and transportation. However, it also raises ethical and societal concerns including job displacement due to automation and privacy issues.
